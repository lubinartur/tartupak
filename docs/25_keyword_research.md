## Takeaways
- The Estonian market is small: dozens to a few hundred searches per keyword per month. Expect a handful of clicks per day, not dozens.
- At 0.3–1 EUR per click a daily budget of about 3–5 EUR is realistic; more will not be spent.
- Wider geography (Latvia, Finland) or English terms are the way to more volume; ask the client where they deliver.
- Not checked: English terms, Latvia, Finland.
